📖 How to play
  1. 3 to 5 players join a room. One is secretly the Impostor.
  2. Normal players see the secret word. The Impostor only sees the category.
  3. Everyone takes turns drawing on the shared canvas (3 turns each, max 10 seconds per turn). One mouse lift ends the turn.
  4. The Impostor sees the canvas the whole time and must bluff their drawings.
  5. After 3 rounds, everyone votes on who the Impostor is.
  6. Caught Impostor: each other player gets +1 point. Hidden Impostor: +3 points.
  7. The Impostor always gets one last chance to guess the word for +1 bonus point.
  8. First to 6 points wins the match.
